Knowledge > Hardware > Laptops
How to request a loaner laptop
KB0031029 Updated Aug 3

If your primary device is being repaired or replaced, you can request a loaner laptop for up to 15 business days. Loaner requests are fulfilled by IT Asset Management from available stock at your site.

Submit a loaner request from the Service Catalog under Hardware. Include your current asset tag and expected return date. Approval is automatic for standard configurations.

Loaner devices are pre-imaged with the standard software bundle. Personal data does not carry over — back up any local files before your device is collected.
